## About

New Nigeria Unity (NNU) is a non-partisan, grassroots peace and unity movement operating across all 36 Nigerian states and the FCT. We believe every Nigerian deserves to live in peace, dignity, and mutual respect — across every ethnicity, faith, and region.

> **Together We Stand. Together We Build Peace. Together We Rise.**
> One Voice. One Nation. One Future.

### Current Campaign

**"One Handshake at a Time"** — launched August 1, 2026. Our flagship *Unity Stories Across Nigeria* series shares real stories from real Nigerians in all 36 states, showing that beneath our differences, we're building the same future.
